About Me关于我

I am a third-year undergraduate student in the School of Computer Science and Technology at Xinjiang University, ranked 9 / 120 in my major and 2 / 120 in overall assessment, with a GPA of 4.12 / 5.0.

我是新疆大学计算机科学与技术学院 2027 届本科生，专业排名 9 / 120，综合测评排名 2 / 120，GPA 为 4.12 / 5.0。

My research focuses on weakly supervised video anomaly detection and multimodal video understanding. I study how to align heterogeneous modalities such as RGB, audio, and optical flow under coarse video-level supervision, so that each modality can correct or strengthen the others when frame-level labels are unavailable.

我的研究方向是弱监督视频异常检测与多模态视频理解，关注在粗粒度视频级监督下，如何对齐 RGB、 音频、光流等异构模态，使不同模态在缺少帧级标注时互相校正并增强判断。

I am actively seeking graduate opportunities for Fall 2026, especially in groups working on video understanding, multimodal learning, and reliable visual intelligence.

目前我正在积极寻求 2026 年秋季研究生阶段机会，尤其关注视频理解、多模态学习与可靠视觉智能方向。
