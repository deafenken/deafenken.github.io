About关于

I am a third-year undergraduate at Xinjiang University (Class of 2027), ranked 9th out of 120 in my major (GPA 4.12/5.0) and 2nd out of 120 in overall assessment. My research centers on weakly supervised video anomaly detection and multimodal video understanding — specifically how to align heterogeneous modalities (RGB, audio, optical flow) under coarse video-level supervision. I am actively seeking graduate opportunities for Fall 2026.
